# How the LLM chose its episodes is stored on the decision, not in two tables of its own

Departs from spec appendix 6, which sketches two tables for a full evaluation with memory: `DECISION_EPISODES` (each past episode the LLM was shown, with its ranks and whether it was opened) and `TOOL_CALLS` (each lookup the LLM made). Both are instead columns of the decision itself, `MEMORY.DECISIONS.EPISODES_SHOWN` and `MEMORY.DECISIONS.LOOKUPS` (migration V9), each a list of records. They are only ever read together with their one decision (the web app showing why the LLM picked what it picked, or an analysis of one evaluation), never queried across decisions on their own. Keeping them on the row means one write per decision, nothing extra to copy when a sim starts from a copy of a memory, and no joins. An analysis across many decisions can still unpack the lists with Snowflake's `FLATTEN`.
