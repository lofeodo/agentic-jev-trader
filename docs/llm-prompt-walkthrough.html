The story in plain words

Every 15 minutes the agent looks at each asset. When Jev (the decision model) says an asset deserves a closer look, the agent runs a full evaluation. Before Jev decides, it gets to read a few records of the agent's own earlier decisions and how they turned out. The LLM's only job is to choose which records. It does not trade.

Past episodeOne stored record: what the market looked like, what Jev decided, how it ended. LongA bet that the price rises. Buy $6,000 of a stock, it rises 1%, you are up $60. ShortA bet that the price falls. It falls 1% on $6,000, you are up $60; it rises 1%, you are down $60. Stop priceThe price at which code closes the trade to cap its loss. Tight is close to the entry price (small loss, easily hit), wide is far away. Candle patternA named shape in the last few price bars ("hammer", "bullish engulfing") that traders read as a hint. Track recordThe scorecard over many closed trades: "42 trades, 17 wins". LookupOne use of a tool that reads memory. The LLM gets 6 per full evaluation. Giving its answer is not a lookup.
