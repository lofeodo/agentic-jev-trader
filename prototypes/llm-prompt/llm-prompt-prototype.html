What the LLM reads, and is told, when it selects past episodes

The question this prototype answers: is this the right wording and the right amount of information for the LLM's one job? Every blue or purple box below is the exact text that would be sent or received. The grey column on the right explains each box in plain words and is not part of any message.

The story in plain words

Every 15 minutes the agent looks at each asset. When Jev (the decision model) says an asset deserves a closer look, the agent runs a full evaluation. Before Jev decides, it gets to read a few records of the agent's own earlier decisions and how they turned out. The LLM's only job is to choose which records. It does not trade.

Past episodeOne stored record: what the market looked like, what Jev decided, how it ended. LongA bet that the price rises. Buy $6,000 of a stock, it rises 1%, you are up $60. ShortA bet that the price falls. It falls 1% on $6,000, you are up $60; it rises 1%, you are down $60. Stop priceThe price at which code closes the trade to cap its loss. Tight is close to the entry price (small loss, easily hit), wide is far away. Candle patternA named shape in the last few price bars ("hammer", "bullish engulfing") that traders read as a hint. Track recordThe scorecard over many closed trades: "42 trades, 17 wins".
